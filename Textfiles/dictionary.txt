• Dictionary:- it is used to store key-value pair under curly braces{}.
• They are mutuable, unordered and don't allow duplicate keys.
• Operations allowed:-
(1)keys():- myDict.keys()
(2)items():- myDict.items()
(3)values():- myDict.values()
(4)get():- myDict.get("key")
(5)update():- myDict.update(ele)
